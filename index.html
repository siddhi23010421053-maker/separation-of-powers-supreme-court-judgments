The cases

Showing 19 of 19 cases

No.CaseIssue before the CourtRuling summary
1Madras Bar Association v. Union of IndiaWhether the Tribunals Reforms Act, 2021 could re-enact provisions previously invalidated by the Court.The Court invalidated key restrictions and held that legislation cannot simply override a judgment without curing the constitutional defect.
2Ashwini Kumar Upadhyay v. Union of India (hate speech)Whether courts may create or expand criminal offences through directions where legislation does not do so.The Court declined the requested intervention, treating creation of criminal offences and punishments as a legislative function.
3Supriyo @ Supriya Chakraborty v. Union of IndiaWhether the Court could extend marriage recognition under the Special Marriage Act to same-sex couples.The Court left creation of a new marriage or civil-union framework to legislative policy.
4Ashwini Kumar Upadhyay v. Union of India (minimum age of marriage)Whether the minimum age of marriage for women should be raised to 21 to match that for men.The Court declined to direct Parliament to establish a uniform minimum age.
5Sanjay Prakash v. Union of India (CAPF, OGAS)Whether executive service and promotion policies in the CAPFs complied with constitutional requirements.The Court used judicial review to address restrictions affecting service equality and highlighted limits on executive action.
6Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited v. Amit GuptaHow the IBC tribunal's jurisdiction applies to disputes involving contracts and regulatory matters.The Court clarifying jurisdictional boundaries among tribunals, contracts, and courts.
7In Re: Special Reference No. 1 of 2025 (Governors, Articles 200 and 201)What limits govern a Governor's options when considering State bills.The Court described limits on gubernatorial inaction and addressed the relationship between executive and legislative roles.
8Anoop Baranwal v. Union of IndiaHow the Chief Election Commissioner and Election Commissioners should be appointed in the absence of legislation.The Court prescribed an interim appointment committee arrangement until Parliament enacted a law.
9Animal Welfare Board of India v. Union of India (Jallikattu)Whether State amendments permitting Jallikattu were valid after the Court's earlier decision.The Court upheld the amendments, treating them as changing the legal framework considered earlier.
10Government of NCT of Delhi v. Union of India (Services)Which government controls services in the National Capital Territory of Delhi.The Court recognized a significant role for Delhi's elected government in administration and discussed federal boundaries.
11Association for Democratic Reforms v. Union of India (Electoral Bonds)Whether the Electoral Bond Scheme and related amendments were constitutionally valid.The Court invalidated the scheme on rights grounds, while recognizing that legislation remains subject to judicial review.
12Cox & Kings Ltd. v. SAP India Pvt. Ltd.How the group-of-companies doctrine applies under Indian arbitration law.The Court addressed interpretation of corporate relationships while applying the governing statutory framework.
13Subhash Desai v. Principal Secretary, Governor of MaharashtraWhether the Governor could call for a trust vote based on internal party disputes and how the Speaker's role applied.The Court found the floor-test decision unlawful but declined to restore the former Chief Minister after his resignation.
14Neil Aurelio Nunes v. Union of India (NEET OBC/EWS)Whether the reservation policy for OBC and EWS candidates in NEET was subject to judicial intervention.The Court upheld the policy and described limits on judicial review of policy choices.
15Pankaj Bansal v. Union of IndiaWhat safeguards apply to arrest powers exercised by enforcement authorities under the PMLA.The Court required written communication of the grounds of arrest as a check on executive power.
16Central Electricity Regulatory Commission v. NTPCHow far courts may intervene in tariff decisions made by expert regulatory bodies.The Court emphasized deference to technical expertise and cautioned against courts setting tariffs.
17S. G. Vombatkere v. Union of India (Sedition)How Section 124A of the Indian Penal Code should be treated while its constitutionality was challenged.The Court stayed enforcement temporarily and asked the Union to reconsider the provision.
18State of Punjab v. Principal Secretary to GovernorWhether the Governor could question the validity of an Assembly session when considering bills passed by it.The Court held that the Governor could not question the session's validity and stressed the functioning of the elected legislature.
19In Re: Section 6A of the Citizenship ActWhether Parliament had competence to enact Section 6A and whether the provision was constitutional.The Court upheld Section 6A and recognized Parliament's latitude in citizenship matters.

State of Jharkhand v. Harihar Mishra is excluded as directed in the project instructions.
